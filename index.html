TELUGU WEDDING TRADITIONS

Moments with meaning.

A little guide to traditions often seen at Telugu weddings. Every family’s ceremony is unique.

జీలకర్ర బెల్లంJeelakarra Bellam

At the muhurtham, the couple place a cumin-and-jaggery mixture on each other’s heads, symbolizing a life shared through every experience.

మాంగల్య ధారణMangalya Dharana

The groom ties the mangalsutra around the bride’s neck, marking their marriage.

తలంబ్రాలుTalambralu

The couple joyfully shower each other with turmeric rice, sharing blessings and laughter.

సప్తపదిSaptapadi

Together, they take seven steps before the sacred fire, each step expressing a promise for their shared life.
